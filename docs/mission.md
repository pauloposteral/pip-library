# Mission and philosophy

Our mission is to make AI-assisted development more transparent, reproducible, collaborative, and accessible.

Our vision is an open ecosystem where people and AI agents collaborate across tools while preserving ownership, permissions, decisions, and verifiable results.

- Evidence over appearance.
- Transparency over exaggerated promises.
- Interoperability over lock-in.
- Human control over hidden automation.
- Open learning and responsible attribution.
- Reproducible work over unverifiable claims.

These principles are operational: a report without a test receipt remains unverified; a configuration file is not a working connection; a roadmap is not a shipping product.
