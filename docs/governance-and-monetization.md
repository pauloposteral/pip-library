# Governance and future commercial services

The public library is intended to remain free. PIP Assurance may offer authenticated evidence and team governance; PIP Cloud may offer hosted infrastructure. These are separate future products, not paid access to upstream Superpowers methodology.

USD is the canonical currency for future commercial specification and research. There are no approved prices in this release. Never relabel old amounts from another currency. Credits, money and provider tokens are distinct units. An external assistant subscription does not authorize resale of model usage.

All commercial capabilities remain disabled in this distribution: no checkout, billing, credits, inference or deployment code is shipped. Before commercial launch independently verify tenant isolation and revocation, server-side billing rights, cost measurement, versioned rate cards, atomic reservations, provider reconciliation, sandbox payment webhooks and idempotency, refunds and applicable tax obligations. Product-specific legal and tax review is not performed by this library.

Before any Build Engine activation, verify isolation, image provenance, scoped provider access, resource limits, cancellation, recovery and rollback. Existing code alone cannot close these gates.

Willingness-to-pay research should interview teams about the cost of release uncertainty, current alternatives and evidence they would trust. Use USD questions and record actual answers only with consent. Do not collect card details, open checkout or announce prices during research. See [the interview guide](growth/interview-guide.md).
