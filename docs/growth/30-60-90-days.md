# 90-day expansion program

Day 1 begins after a verified public release, not after candidate creation. Every number below is a hypothesis until measured.

| Window | Target deliverables | Measurement and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Public v0.1.0, 3 skills, 4 Markdown templates plus manifest, 3 client guides, 2 examples, first tutorials, at least 5 structured interviews | Observe actual installation success, first useful outcome, questions and friction |
| Days 31–60 | 10 useful examples, independently verified Codex/Claude/Copilot paths, contribution process, compatibility matrix and release cadence | Track attempted/successful installs, external issues and meaningful contributions |
| Days 61–90 | Repeat-use observations, at least 1 independently reproduced QA workflow, Assurance concept validation and USD price research | Revisit roadmap using observed demand and measured infrastructure cost |

## Adoption journey

Discover through accurate GitHub topics, documentation, useful technical articles and eligible directories. Understand the problem in a short README. Try a template or a verified public information call without a paid signup. Improve through small iterations, questions and contributions. Share reproducible examples with attribution. Return for fixes, compatibility notes and new examples. Consider a paid hosted product only after a real service exists.

Posteral owns initial weekly triage and monthly roadmap review. Cadence is a proposal, not a scheduled automation. No outreach has been sent. Do not buy stars, invent reviews, post unsolicited bulk PRs or count repository stars as paying customers.

Metrics remain in [metrics.json](metrics.json). Record source, timestamp, denominator and consent. Unknown adoption values remain null. Optional analytics requires separate privacy configuration and consent; no tracking is installed in this release.
