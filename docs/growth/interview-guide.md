# Structured user interview

Recruit volunteers through appropriate opt-in channels. Ask consent before recording; do not request private code, prompts, credentials or client identities. Use anonymous notes and a retention decision.

1. Describe the last time you could not tell whether AI-generated work was ready to ship.
2. What did you do to verify it? What time or money did that consume?
3. Try one library template while explaining where you hesitate. Observe completion; do not coach success.
4. Which evidence would make you trust a release report? Which claims would you challenge?
5. Would authenticated, commit-linked evidence help your team? What alternative would you use?
6. If a real hosted evidence service existed, what monthly amount in USD would feel inexpensive, reasonable, expensive and too expensive? These answers are research, not prices or purchase commitments.
7. May we contact you once for follow-up? Record that choice separately.

Record participant code, voluntary role/experience, date, attempted workflow, actual outcome, friction, exact permitted quotes and follow-up consent. Target: five interviews in the first 30 days. Completed interviews in this preparation: zero. No willingness-to-pay claims exist yet.
